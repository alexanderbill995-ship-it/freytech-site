## Q1. WAPOTECH does not make a supplemental-treatment product

**Request:** "WAPOTECH — under supplemental."

**What we found.** WAPOTECH INC. (Warwick, Rhode Island, wapotech.com) — and the related Austrian
WAPOTEC GmbH, note the different spelling — sells **no UV, no ozone, and no AOP product at all**.
Their catalog is:
- Dosed treatment chemicals — HydroSan flocculation aid, HydroXan oxidation aid, WAPO Floc22 coagulant
- Sand and multi-media filters — WAPO TEC, 2 to 10 ft diameter
- WAPO Chlor on-site salt electrolysis, generating 0.6% sodium hypochlorite. That is a **primary
  sanitiser**, not a supplement.
- ClearAmine, an **air-quality monitor** for trichloramine. It measures; it does not treat.

The only item that is genuinely supplemental is **HydroXan**, a chemical oxidation aid used
alongside chlorine — a chemical, not a device.

**Why it matters.** Filing an electrolysis chlorine generator or a sand filter under "supplemental
treatment" is the kind of mismatch that gets a specification rejected on review.

**Recommended answer:** list WAPOTECH under **filtration** (the WAPO TEC filters) and/or
**chemical delivery and chlorination** (WAPO Chlor). Tell us which parts of their line you actually
carry and we will file them correctly. Nothing has been published yet.

## Q2. ASC Pumping Equipment is a distributor, and its territory does not include New York

**Request:** "Manufacturers Represented — ASC Pumping Equipment, Inc.-Aurora / Grundfos / Pentair."

**What we found.** ASC describes itself in its own words as *"a sales and service organization"*
and *"a full service distributor"* — not a manufacturer. It was incorporated in 1984 by buying the
**Wisconsin sales territory** from Aurora Pump Company. Its three offices are West Bend WI,
Appleton WI and Lenexa KS, and its published territory is Wisconsin, the Upper Peninsula of
Michigan, Kansas and western Missouri. **New York is not in it.**

Aurora itself is straightforward and verified: **Aurora is a Pentair brand** (aurorapump.com
redirects to Pentair; Pentair acquired Aurora in 1997). So Aurora and Pentair are one relationship,
not two.

**Recommended answer:** confirm which is true —
(a) ASC is a **supplier you buy through**, in which case it does not belong on the site as a
    manufacturer, and we list **Aurora (a Pentair brand)** instead; or
(b) something else we should understand.

One caution on Aurora: the only Aurora brochure written specifically for pools is from **2013** and
the 340 Series it covers is now **discontinued**. The current commercial pool offering is the
**3800 Series** end-suction and **410 Series** split-case pumps. We would list those.

## Q3. E-Z Clor has no commercial line, and its lead product conflicts with New York rules

**Request:** "Here is info on the specialty chemicals. EZ Clor Chemical Product Brochure / Jacks Magic."

Jack's Magic is already on the site and stays — seven products. E-Z Clor has three problems:

1. **It is a distributor private brand, not a manufacturer.** Every page on ezclorchemicals.com
   carries "© 2026 Pool Corporation" — POOLCORP, the wholesale distributor behind SCP, Superior
   Pool Products and Horizon.
2. **There is no commercial line.** The entire site is written for residential pool owners.
   Published pack sizes stop at 35 lb tabs, 25 lb granular, 1 gallon liquid. The 50 lb commercial
   pails exist only on third-party retail sites.
3. **The brochure you sent is not a manufacturer document.** A full archive crawl of their site
   turns up only safety data sheets — no brochure, catalogue or line card has ever been published
   there. Yours is almost certainly a POOLCORP sales-centre piece. **Where did it come from?**

**The one that actually matters.** E-Z Clor's lead sanitisers are **trichlor tablets**. Trichlor
adds cyanuric acid, and our own compliance page states that 10 NYCRR 6-1.11 prohibits cyanuric acid
in New York public pools. Publishing trichlor into a New York commercial catalogue would
contradict the regulatory position the rest of the site takes.

**Recommended answer:** list the E-Z Clor **supplements only** — clarifiers, enzymes, phosphate
removers, stain and scale products — which is what "specialty chemicals" means on this site anyway,
and leave the trichlor sanitisers off. Confirm that is what you intended, and send the brochure's
origin so we can source the facts properly.

The bulkhead gap is the one worth a second thought: your Ithaca College project is a bulkhead job,
and it is still on the site as a reference.
