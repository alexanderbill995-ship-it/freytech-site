## How it works (manual Sec. 2.1, verbatim)
1. "Pulsar® Plus Briquettes are placed in a basket and suspended over the solution reservoir."
2. "The control box is plugged into a chemical controller output and powered on."
3. "When the chemical controller requires chlorine, the feed solenoid valve opens allowing water from the pool return to enter the inlet water manifold at the proper supply flow rate."
4. "Low-velocity, high-volume water enters the reservoir and covers the bottom of the basket."
5. "Pulsar® Plus Briquettes erode into the reservoir and create a consistent 0.3% available chlorine solution with 84 °F [28.8 °C] inlet water temperature."
6. "The chlorinated solution fills the reservoir, lifting the float switch connected to the discharge valve until the valve opens."
7. "The discharge valve and outlet tubing are connected to a Venturi. The vacuum generated by the Venturi suction drains the reservoir tank and sends the concentrated solution into the pool return."

Key difference from full-size Precision: **no dedicated booster pump is part of the standard Precision 30** — it is "Flow Based" (Figure 1: "Pulsar® Precision 30 Flow Based Installation"), drawing motive water from a Venturi bypass loop off the pool return "downstream of the filter and heater". A booster-pump variant appears in installation schematics ("GuardTec, Precision 30, Acid Plus (with booster pump)").

### FLAGGED INCONSISTENCIES
- **Lower threshold**: product page says 10,000 gal minimum; category/index pages say "over 100K / 100,000 gallons" for the cal-hypo line as a whole. These conflict by an order of magnitude.
- **Upper threshold**: product page says both "up to 100,000 gallons" and "up to 300,000 gallons" (title, H1, spec line, and one body sentence say 300K; one body sentence says 100K).
- **Manual vs web**: the manual's maximum is 200,000 gal (indoor) / 150,000 (outdoor stabilized) / 90,000 (outdoor non-stabilized) — lower than the web's 300,000 and explicitly "for guidance only".
- Recommended publishing language: "Manufacturer-rated for commercial pools up to ~300,000 gallons (site-dependent); Pulsar's installation manual gives application-specific guidance of up to 90,000 gal outdoor non-stabilized, 150,000 gal outdoor stabilized, 200,000 gal indoor, and 15,000 gal spas, based on nominal bather loads." Avoid stating a firm lower bound; or say "from smaller commercial pools" without a number.

## Chlorine output capacity (manual Table 12, verbatim)
> "Chlorine Delivery Rate (Pools) 2 – 36 lbs [0.9 kg – 16.3 kg] AvCl/day — Maximum of 1.5 lbs [0.68 kg] AvCl/hr"
> "Chlorine Delivery Rate (Spas) 2.5 – 42 lbs [1.13 kg – 19.05 kg] AvCl/day — Maximum of 1.75 lbs [0.79 kg] AvCl/hr"
> "Solution Strength 0.2 – 0.3% at 84 °F [28.8 °C] inlet water temperature"
> "Water Inlet 1.25 gpm [4.7 lpm] @ 5 psi [0.34 bar] filter pressure"
> "Venturi Discharge Rate 1.4 gpm [5.3 lpm] @ 3 psi [0.21 bar] differential on Venturi"

Table 13 (Pools, inlet flow vs AvCl lbs/day): 0.3 gpm → 2 (25%); 0.6 → 18 (50%); 0.9 → 26 (75%); 1.25 → 36 (100%).
Table 14 (Spas): 0.3 → 2.5; 0.6 → 21; 0.9 → 32; 1.25 → 42 lbs/day.
Startup: "Slowly open the feeder inlet 1/2″ ball valve until the flow meter reads 1.25 gpm max." Inlet flow above 1.25 gpm is not permitted.

## Other inconsistencies within the manual
- Clearance: Sec 4.1.1 gives 48×48×76 in recommended / 36×36×64 in minimum (identical to the full-size Precision manual — likely copied), while Appendix Table 9 gives 44×30×54 in recommended and a "minimum clearance" equal to the feeder's own dimensions (19.5×15.5×34). Use the Appendix values and say "per manufacturer manual".
- Cable length typo: "The single DIN extension cable is 9.8 ft [93 m]" (should be 3 m; Table 11 says 9.8′ [3 m]).
- Solution strength: 0.3% in overview vs 0.2–0.3% in spec table.
