## How it works (manual, Section 2.1 "Overview of Operation", verbatim)
Source: Pulsar® Precision Operation and Installation Manual (Model PS-1HCE) Rev 1.0 4/2020. Official download is linked from https://pulsarpools.com/support-and-manuals/ (Salesforce-hosted link: https://solenis.my.salesforce.com/sfc/p/#50000000JWVF/a/UX000009XmlR/AGSzW8GKSEdv0kGlw1IpZXic7BKoshSkzHpzdOL5n9Y). Text below was read from a copy hosted by a Pulsar dealer (SECONDARY HOST, same document): https://www.streamlineaquatics.com/Customer-Content/www/CMS/files/Pulsar_Precision_Operation_and_Installation_Manual.pdf
1. "Pulsar® Plus Briquettes are placed in a basket and suspended over the solution reservoir."
2. "The control box is plugged into a chemical controller output and powered on."
3. "When the chemical controller requires chlorine, the feed solenoid valve opens allowing water from the pool return to enter the inlet water manifold at the proper supply flow rate."
4. "Low-velocity, high-volume water enters the reservoir and covers the bottom of the basket."
5. "Pulsar® Plus Briquettes erode into the reservoir and create a consistent 0.7% available chlorine solution with 84 °F [28.8 °C] inlet water temperature."
6. "The chlorinated solution fills the reservoir, lifting the float switch connected to the discharge valve until the valve opens."
7. "The discharge valve and outlet tubing are connected to a venturi. The vacuum generated by the venturi suction drains the reservoir tank and sends the concentrated solution into the pool return."

Plain-language summary for copy: dry briquettes sit in a hopper/basket; on a chlorine-demand signal from the chemistry controller, filtered pool water is sprayed/flooded across the bed, eroding a controlled layer into a ~0.6–0.8% available-chlorine solution in a reservoir; a float-actuated discharge valve releases the solution to a booster-pump-driven Venturi, which injects it into the pool return line.

Cal-hypo systems page (https://pulsarpools.com/products/cal-hypo-feeder-systems/) wording:
> "Dry Cal Hypo briquettes stay dry in the hopper until water sprays across the bed and dissolves a measured layer into solution. That solution feeds to the pool, so chlorine output follows water flow and pool demand."

## Safety / handling notes (manual Section 1, paraphrased with key verbatim)
- Calcium hypochlorite is a strong oxidizer: "Do not allow Pulsar® Plus Briquettes to come into contact with any foreign matter … acids, organics, nitrogen-containing compounds, dry powder fire [extinguishers] …" — "may cause a violent reaction that produces heat, an explosion, a [fire]".
- "This system contains liquid under pressure and calcium hypochlorite solution can [cause burns]…" — PPE required; avoid contact with solution or vapor.
- Clean up spills without delay; refer to the SDS.
- Electrical work "must be performed by a licensed electrician in accordance with the National Electric Code (NEC)".
- Use only Pulsar® Plus Briquettes (warranty/safety).
- Do not dump briquettes (dust); fill with a scoop; "Dustless Procedure" provided.
- Periodic hopper/base cleaning; acid cleaning of base with muriatic acid or Pulsar® Acid Cleaner 50 (with caution about mixing acid and cal hypo residues).

## Inconsistencies / cautions
1. **Gallon range framing differs by page**: product page = 500K to 1M+; cal-hypo category page and products index = "commercial pools over 100K/100,000 gallons" (an umbrella statement for both Precision models). Use "500K to 1M+ (manufacturer guidance)" for the full-size unit and cite the product page.
2. **Solution strength**: overview text says "0.7% available chlorine solution"; spec table says "0.6 – 0.8%". Publish the range.
3. **Water inlet**: Table 5 says 2.5–3.5 gpm @ 30–40 psig; the Venturi discharge is 3 gpm nominal — both are manual values, not contradictory, but note they are for the feeder loop, not pool circulation.
4. **Manual date**: Rev 1.0 4/2020 is the newest revision found; it predates the 2023 GuardTec integration (feeder control module). Electrical/control-box details may differ for GuardTec-bundled units.
5. **NSF standard number not named** anywhere on manufacturer pages for feeders.
6. **Ownership names**: Lonza (2017 legacy manuals) → Sigura (2020) → Solenis / Innovative Water Care, LLC (2023 manuals, website). Use "Pulsar® Systems (Solenis)" only if you also keep the trademark attribution.
