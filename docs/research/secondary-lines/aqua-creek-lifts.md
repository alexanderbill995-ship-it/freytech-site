## ADA / UL statements — manufacturer's exact wording
- Lift Guide "UNIQUE BENEFITS": **"ADA COMPLIANT — The first pool lift manufacturer to have our lifts independently verified to meet ADA regulations."**
- **"UL CERTIFIED — We carry the largest selection of UL Certified pool lifts available on the market today."**
- **"SOLAR POWER — The first and only manufacturer in North America to offer solar compatible pool lifts."**
- Product pages use the fixed phrase **"ADA compliant & UL safety certified"** (Scout Excel, Mighty 400/600, Mighty Voyager, Revolution XL) or "ADA Compliant & UL Certified" (Admiral, Ranger 2, Lift Guide). Aqua Creek's public pages do **not** cite the UL standard number; do not add "UL 60335-2-1000" unless taken from a spec sheet.
- Homepage framing: "Creating an accessible aquatic facility goes beyond meeting ADA requirements." (https://aquacreekproducts.com/)
- Aqua Creek news post on the ADA deadline references "sections 242 and 1009 of the 2010 Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) Standards for Accessible Design" (https://aquacreekproducts.com/apsp-urges-owners-operators-of-pools-spas-to-prepare-for-upcoming-ada-deadline/).
