## BECSys CO₂ Feed Solutions — brochure SLS-6107-B
PDF: https://www.becsys.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/09/SLS-6107-B_CO2.pdf (linked from https://www.becsys.com/products/ ; supersedes SLS-6107-A at /wp-content/uploads/2021/06/SLS-6107-A_CO2.pdf). Footer: "Document #6107-B"; contact BECSysInfo@becs.com, www.becs.com.

Verbatim key facts:
- "The BECSys CO₂ Feed System is a rugged and reliable solution for aquatic facilities. CO₂ is widely recognized as a safe and easy-to-use method for pH control."
- Sizes: **"Two sizes are available: 5-50 SCFH, appropriate for most applications, and 20-200 SCFH, where high volumes of CO₂ are required."**
- Injection: "Customers may also choose between a standard diffuser for CO₂ injection and an optional venturi eductor." Power: "115 VAC and 230 VAC versions are both available."
- Alkalinity rationale: "CO₂ also assists in maintaining Total Alkalinity (TA). Pools that use exclusively acid (which rapidly depletes alkalinity) for pH control will find it very difficult to maintain TA at acceptable levels, which leads to erratic pH and inferior water quality."
- Controller integration: "BECSys7 and BECSys5 controllers, equipped with a BECSys Alkalinity Meter, will monitor pool TA and automatically select between acid and CO₂ for pH control based upon operator-defined TA set points. In most pools routine bicarb or acid additions to maintain alkalinity will no longer be necessary."
- Features bullets: "Safe and effective pH control"; **"NSF Certified and Listed to the requirements of NSF/ANSI 50 using NSF CCS-14530"**; "1 year manufacturer's warranty"; "Provides optimal performance with all BECSys controllers"; "Full line of compatible CO₂ accessories also available".
- Accessories (p.2): **Pressure Regulator** — "Forged brass body and housing cap with large 2" gauges", "Maximum inlet pressure 3000 PSIG", "Delivery range 4-80 PSIG". **CO₂ Heater** — prevents supply lines freezing in high-volume applications; "120VAC and 240VAC versions available". **CO₂ Tank Switchovers** — "Choose from Automatic or Manual tank switchovers"; "With either switchover CO₂ feed continues so the exhausted tank can be changed on your schedule".
- Company blurb: "BECS Technology has been designing and manufacturing the world's most reliable and innovative water chemistry controllers for nearly 30 years." "BECSys distributors have been factory-trained and authorized…"

Related BECS literature on the same products page: Alkalinity Monitoring & Control SLS-6095-E (https://www.becsys.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/10/SLS-6095-E_Alkalinity_Meter.pdf); BECSys5 SLS-4333-H; BECSys7 SLS-4335-F; BECSys ChemLock SLS-6152-A.
