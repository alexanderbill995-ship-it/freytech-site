## 2. What the Defender is

- Product page description: perlite regenerative media filtration, "filtering particulate down to 1 micron and require no backwash". Category page: https://www.xylem.com/en-us/catalog/products--services/filtration-systems/defender-regenerative-media-filters/ ; product page: https://www.xylem.com/en-us/catalog/products--services/filtration-systems/defender-regenerative-media-filters/defender-regenerative-media-filter/
- Core construction (family brochure p.3): "Each flextube is built from 316 stainless steel … Encasing the stainless core is a porous polymer membrane"; "Hundreds of flextubes create substantial filter surface area"; perlite is "a lightweight, porous volcanic glass expanded by heat into sharp-edged, irregular particles" applied as a precoat.
- Operating cycle (brochure p.2): (1) **Precoat** — "water recirculates through the Defender for 10 minutes … forms a uniform layer of perlite media on the flextubes"; (2) **Filter mode**; (3) **Bump cycle** — "Typically once a day, a pneumatic mechanism rapidly deflates and re-inflates, creating a 'bump' that dislodges perlite and captured debris"; (4) **Media change** — "after several weeks of operation … the fouled perlite is flushed to sewer."
- **Pressure vs. vacuum — correction for the brief:** the Defender is a **pressure-vessel** RMF. The manual's own definition: "Diatomite-type Filter – A pressure or vacuum type device designed to filter water through a thin layer of filter aid" (O&M p.7), and Defender is the pressure form (inlet/outlet flanged pressure vessel, 16" clearance above filter for service, per TDS). "Vacuum" in Defender literature refers to the **perlite Vacuum Transfer Unit** used to load media (O&M TOC: "Vacuum Transfer Unit … 32"; "Loading Perlite-Vacuum Transfer … 50"). Do not describe the Defender as a "vacuum filter".
- Media guidance (Xylem FAQ): "We recommend a very specific Harborlite® perlite"; "Most NSF50 approved perlites will likely work in the Defender filter"; change media "when the differential pressure of the filter is at 10psi or greater after a bump cycle". https://www.xylem.com/en-us/resources/faqs/top-5-defender-regenerative-media-filter-faqs/
- Operating FAQs: "Defender filters may be installed on 70 – 2500 GPM (single filter) pools and spas."; regeneration "will take no longer than 10 minutes"; "The complete filter cycle using the same charge of perlite usually averages 4 weeks depending on bather load."; media change "takes 15 – 25 minutes"; "Every Defender system is designed to be fully automatic but is operable in semi-automatic mode." https://www.xylem.com/en-us/resources/faqs/general-faqs-for-defender-filters/

## 3. Manufacturer-stated benefits (quote conservatively — all are "up to" claims)

Family brochure (XYL-DEFENDER-BR-US-0126):
- "Up to 75% savings IN SPACE" — "The footprint of Defender filters can be up to 75% smaller than equivalently sized sand filters"
- "Up to 90% reduction IN WATER AND SEWER" — "Defender filters do not require frequent backwashing like sand filters"; "Eliminates backwash holding tank"; "Enables smaller waste line to sewer"
- "Up to 50% savings IN ENERGY, FUEL AND CHEMICALS" — lower pressure drop, no re-heating/re-treating of backwash makeup water
- "Facilities typically see an ROI of 3-5 years, with some facilities seeing cost savings in less than one year."
- Infographic: "Filtration efficiency, 5-micron particles — 99% Defender / 28% Sand" (brochure marks this "for informational purposes only").
- Product page tiles: "90% Water Savings", "50% Energy Savings", "30% Chemical Savings", "75% Space Savings", "95% Less Particulate". Virtuo datasheet: "Up to 90% water savings, 50% energy savings, 30% chemical savings, 75% footprint savings".

**Recommended phrasing for the Frey catalog:** "Manufacturer-stated savings versus high-rate sand: up to 90% water/sewer, up to 50% energy, up to 30% chemicals, up to 75% footprint; filters to 1 micron; no backwash — daily automatic 'bump' regeneration with a perlite change roughly every few weeks depending on bather load."
