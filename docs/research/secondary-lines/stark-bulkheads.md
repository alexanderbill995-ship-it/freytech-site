## Materials / construction summary for catalog copy
- Material: fiberglass-reinforced composite ("performance composite materials"; press release: "custom designed composite bulkheads"). One-piece (monolithic) moulding — not modular/bolted sections.
- Movement: air-chamber (buoyancy) system — the bulkhead is inflated to float and walked into position, then re-anchored; no rollers or deck tracks.
- Use cases: dividing a 50 m pool into short-course/long-course configurations, water-polo fields, simultaneous programming (lessons, lap swim, team practice), meet hosting; starting-block anchor mounts and timing-system compatibility.
